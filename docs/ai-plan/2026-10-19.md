## Cycle 1 → v12.0.20 "Submarine warfare: sonar & ASW" (v12.1: deepen basic subs)
Today each side's sub is a grey cylinder that slides left/right at a fixed depth, pops to periscope depth every
30–55 s and fires one torpedo that nearly always hits. Nothing can find or fight it, and nobody hears anything.

New module `js/theaters/asw.js` (`LB.asw`, kill switch `?asw=0` → the v12.0 ambush subs):
1. **Hull:** an attack-sub model in our own procedural geometry: long round hull with tapered bow and stern, a sail
   with sail planes, cruciform tail fins and a shrouded propulsor. Faction stripe on the sail. Periscope/mast raised
   only at periscope depth, with a small feather wake.
2. **Tactics:** patrol deep and quiet → stalk toward the nearest enemy surface group → come to periscope depth for a
   firing solution (6 s) → fire a wire-guided heavyweight torpedo → go deep and sprint away (noisy) → patrol. Hit or
   chased, it evades (deep, fast, noisemaker decoy).
3. **Sonar (passive + active):** every sailing destroyer listens. Detection range grows with the sub's noise (quiet
   patrol 18 m, sprinting 42 m, periscope depth adds a 45 m radar/visual "periscope" detection) and shrinks in rain
   (sea noise ×0.8). A destroyer that holds a weak contact goes active: an expanding sonar ping ring on the water and a
   synthesized ping (+echo) sound, which firms up the contact out to 55 m. Contacts carry a quality (0–1) that decays
   when nobody hears the sub.
4. **ASW attack:** a destroyer with a firm contact closes in and fires (ASROC-style lightweight torpedo when > 14 m,
   a depth-charge pattern when close). Hits depend on contact quality and sub depth; underwater blasts throw up white
   water domes. A sub at 0 hp breaks up (bubbles, oil slick, "breaking-up noises") and a replacement arrives 60 s later.
5. **Torpedo defence:** torpedoes now run at a realistic slower 18 m/s with a 14 s run. A ship that hears one calls
   "torpedo in the water", turns away, and may stream a decoy that seduces it (~35 %). Hits still go through the
   existing theater strike path.
6. **Tactical map:** subsurface symbols (open-top frame) labelled SSN. Own subs always; enemy subs only while held as a
   sonar contact (dashed when the contact is weak); ping rings on the big map.
7. **Commander:** Map → WAR SYSTEMS · ASW panel shows own sub state/hp/torpedoes, contacts and kills; buttons for
   "Sonar ping" (your destroyers go active now, 20 s cooldown) and "Sub: hunt here" (sends your sub to the camera point,
   key `J`). Radio lines with naval brevity ("Conn, sonar, new contact…", "Torpedo in the water!", "Weapon away").
8. Tier cost: decoy/bubble puffs and ping rings fewer on LOW/phone; one sub per side everywhere.
Game/sim layer only (SIMULATED): never touches market data, army counts, the front or data-truth labels.
