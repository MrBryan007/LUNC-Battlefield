## Cycle 1 → v12.0.17 "UAV recon & strike drones" (v12.1: deepen basic drones + realism gap: brevity codes)
Today each side's UAVs are three grey boxes flying a fixed circle. They give fog-of-war vision only when fog is on, and
they never spot, strike, run out of weapons or get shot down.

New module `js/theaters/uav.js` (`LB.uav`, kill switch `?uav=0` → the v12.0 orbiting boxes):
1. **Airframe:** a MALE (medium-altitude, long-endurance) drone model in our own procedural geometry — long straight
   wing, slender fuselage with a sensor ball under the nose, V-tail, pusher prop that spins, nav lights. Banks in turns.
2. **Tasking:** each drone flies to a task point and orbits it (radius ~22 m, at 30–36 m). The side's AI re-tasks every
   25–40 s between the land front, the enemy airbase, enemy ships and enemy depots/defenses. The Commander can task
   their side's drone to the current camera point (Map → WAR SYSTEMS · UAV, and the `U` key).
3. **Sensor:** a sensor footprint (36 m, ×0.6 in fog, ×0.8 in rain, IR so night doesn't cut it) spots enemy ships,
   jets on the ground, defenses and depots. Spotted contacts get a SPOT marker on the tactical map for 20 s and a radio
   call with a grid reference. fow already takes drone vision; the drone positions it reads stay correct.
4. **Strike:** two missiles per sortie. When a spotted target is in range and the 40–60 s cooldown is up, the drone fires
   ("RIFLE"), the missile flies a visible smoke-trailed pursuit and hits through the existing `LB.theaters.onStrike`
   (which already damages ships, parked jets, defenses and depots) at small power, then "SPLASH". When empty
   ("WINCHESTER") it flies back to its airbase, rearms for 20 s and relaunches.
5. **Threat:** enemy AA (airbase AA, base AA defenses, sailing warships) can shoot a drone down when it orbits inside
   their envelope (per-second roll, weather-scaled). It spirals down trailing smoke, crashes with a puff, and a new one
   launches from its airbase 40 s later. "Reaper down" radio line.
6. Tacmap: drones drawn as air frames with a UAV label; own drones show their sensor ring on the large map.
7. Tier cost: 1 drone per side on LOW/phone, 2 on MEDIUM+; trails and nav lights off on LOW.
Game/sim layer only (SIMULATED): never touches market data, army counts, the front or data-truth labels.

## Cycle 2 → v12.0.18 "Weather & cyber warfare affect combat" (v12.1: weather affecting accuracy + cyber effects on radar/AA)
Today weather is cosmetic (rain particles, shorter fog range) and a cyber hack is a screen glitch plus a radar blind
in fow.js. Nothing in combat cares.

New module `js/theaters/wx-combat.js` (`LB.wxCombat`, kill switch `?wxcombat=0`): one table of condition modifiers that
the combat code asks.
1. **Weather & night → combat:** naval gunnery spread (rain ×1.35, fog ×1.7, night ×1.15; radar-laid guns lose less
   than eyes), theater + base AA hit chance (rain ×0.75, fog ×0.55, night ×0.85), IR air-to-air missile hits (rain ×0.8,
   fog ×0.65 → more misses), direct-fire range of base MG / sentry / sniper positions (rain ×0.82, fog ×0.55, night ×0.75).
2. **Cyber → systems:** each hack (the v12.0 random pulse, or a Commander-ordered one) also takes one victim system
   offline for 15 s: AA guns (theater AA + base AA defenses stop firing), UAV links (drones keep orbiting but can't
   spot or fire), or comms (the player's tactical-map inset shows a COMMS JAMMED static overlay). fow.js keeps its radar blind.
3. **Commander:** Map → WAR SYSTEMS · WEATHER & CYBER shows the live modifiers and every outage per side, with
   Counter-hack (5 intel: restore everything, including radar, and a 30 s firewall) and Cyber attack (8 intel, 60 s
   cooldown) paid from the war economy.
4. **Rain realism:** the ground soaks over ~20 s (darker, glossier) and dries over ~45 s, with small splash puffs near
   the camera on MEDIUM+.

## Cycle 3 → v12.0.19 "Fires that burn out over minutes" (realism gap: FX) + LOW black-disc bug
Bug found while checking the cycle-2 phone screenshots: on LOW / phones the crater decals render as soft BLACK discs
along the front. Cause: three r128 `InstancedMesh.setColorAt()` allocates `instanceColor` from `this.count * 3`, and
`js/fx/craters.js` set `count = 0` before its setColorAt loop, so the colour buffer was empty and every decal drew with
a zero colour. Fix: allocate the instance colour buffer for the whole pool first.

Realism gap: fires are 5–20 s flame zones that just stop. New `js/fx/fires.js` (`LB.fires`, kill switch `?fires=0`):
1. Every lasting fire (burning tank wrecks, crashed aircraft, napalm / fire strikes, critical vehicle fires, wreck
   husks) becomes a fire site with a lifecycle: burn 50–110 s with flames that shrink as the fuel burns down and a dark
   oily smoke column that greys out → smoulder 70–140 s (embers + a thin grey wisp that fades) → out, leaving a charred
   scorch through the crater system. Sites close together merge (re-fuel) instead of stacking columns.
2. One slowly veering wind (0.6–1.4 m/s) drifts every column the same way. Rain shortens burns ×0.6 and turns smoke
   into pale steam.
3. Budget: per-tier site caps (LOW 8 … ULTRA 36), nearest sites at full rate, far ones at a quarter rate (smoke only),
   nothing beyond 160 m, and emission backs off when the shared sprite pool is over 70 % so explosions never starve.
   MEDIUM+ get 2–4 flickering fire lights created once at start (no light-count shader recompiles later).
Visual layer only: never touches market data, armies, the front or labels.
