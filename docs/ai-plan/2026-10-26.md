# AI build plan — 2026-10-26

## Cycle 1 → v12.0.37 "SAM sites: radar-guided missiles" (realism checklist, aircraft: "Still open: SAM sites / radar-guided missiles")
The air theater has gun AA (flak since v12.0.34) and IR dogfight missiles, but no surface-to-air missiles. New module `js/fx/sam.js`:
1. Each side gets a SAM battery north of its airbase (MEDIUM and up add a second one behind the HQ): a search radar that
   rotates, a fire-control radar that slews onto its target, and a launcher truck with 4 canisters that elevates and turns.
2. Engagement cycle: search (range about 240 m) → track and lock (about 2 s, longer in rain, fog or at night through the
   wxCombat 'aa' modifier) → launch → reload. A cyber hack on AA takes the radars down. Minimum altitude, minimum and
   maximum range. Shoot-look-shoot: 6 s between shots, 4 rounds then a 25 s reload.
3. The missile boosts to about 120 m/s, flies proportional navigation (N about 3.5) with a turn-rate limit, burns out and
   slows, and self-destructs after about 6 s. Proximity fuze about 5 m, lethal inside about 2.5 m, fragment damage on the
   same jet hit points that flak uses (damaged jets smoke and RTB).
4. The target reacts: radar warning ("mud spike"), a beam turn to notch the radar, and chaff. Chaff and notching can break
   the lock, and the missile then flies on unguided. Radio on your own side (SIMULATED) with real brevity words.
5. FX: launch flash and backblast cloud, a thick white booster trail (lighter on LOW and phones), burst flash and puff.
   Missiles in flight capped per tier. API `sam.getState()`. Kill switch `?sam=0`. Theater FX only, labelled SIMULATED;
   never touches market data, army counts, the price-driven front or data-truth labels.

Result (cycle 1): all gates passed; deployed as v12.0.37. Changed in testing: guidance never engaged (fixed the early target-gone check), both battery sites moved to open ground.

## Cycle 2 → v12.0.38 "Sea surface: whitecaps, rolling breakers and wet sand" (realism checklist, sea + terrain + materials:
"no foam on wave crests and shoreline breakers are a pulsing strip", "shoreline foam upgrade", "wet shoreline PBR")
New module `js/fx/shoreline.js`, hooked from the theaters ocean build:
1. Whitecaps: foam on the wave crests from the ocean shader's own wave height, broken up by drifting noise, more of it
   in rain. Shallow water near the beach shades lighter and greener, deep water stays dark.
2. Breakers: the flat pulsing foam strip is replaced by a wider surf band where sets of foam lines roll in toward the
   beach, break up along the shore and wash up the sand and back (swash).
3. Wet sand: a darker, glossier band of sand along the waterline that follows the swash; rain wets the whole beach.
4. LOW and phones (flat ocean) keep the shallow-water shading, breakers and wet sand, without crest foam.
5. Kill switch `?shore=0` (also off with `?realism=0` / `?seaair=0`). Cosmetic only, nothing touches market data.

Result (cycle 2): all gates passed; deployed as v12.0.38. Changed in testing: smaller whitecaps, fainter swash film over darker wet sand, the swell damped near the beach to stop it cutting through the sand.

## Cycle 3 → v12.0.39 "Positional battle audio: stereo position, air absorption, sound delay, Doppler" (realism checklist,
audio: "Doppler on more than jets", "positional gunfire HRTF-ish pan")
Battle sounds only knew "how far", and several newer systems passed metres where `sfx` expects 0–1, so their booms were
pinned at the quietest level (flak, SAM, the ridge, ASW). In `js/sfx.js`:
1. Optional world position on `explosion(power, dist, pos)` and `gunfire(dist, pos)`: stereo pan from the camera's right
   vector, a duller sound from behind (simple head-shadow high cut), air absorption (a low-pass that closes with
   distance), and the speed-of-sound delay scheduled on the audio clock (unless the caller already delays it).
2. Distances above 1 are treated as metres and normalised, which fixes the near-silent booms.
3. New `whoosh(pos, vel, power)`: a passing-object roar with a Doppler pitch shift from the radial speed; used for SAM
   launches. Positions wired into the battle-engine impacts and small-arms fire, realism-pass far blasts, flak, SAM, forts,
   the ridge, base invasions and ASW. Kill switch `?spatial=0` (old flat behaviour). Audio only.
