# AI build plan — 2026-10-26

## Cycle 1 → v12.0.37 "SAM sites: radar-guided missiles" (realism checklist, aircraft: "Still open: SAM sites / radar-guided missiles")
The air theater has gun AA (flak since v12.0.34) and IR dogfight missiles, but no surface-to-air missiles. New module `js/fx/sam.js`:
1. Each side gets a SAM battery north of its airbase (MEDIUM and up add a second one behind the HQ): a search radar that
   rotates, a fire-control radar that slews onto its target, and a launcher truck with 4 canisters that elevates and turns.
2. Engagement cycle: search (range about 240 m) → track and lock (about 2 s, longer in rain, fog or at night through the
   wxCombat 'aa' modifier) → launch → reload. A cyber hack on AA takes the radars down. Minimum altitude, minimum and
   maximum range. Shoot-look-shoot: 6 s between shots, 4 rounds then a 25 s reload.
3. The missile boosts to about 120 m/s, flies proportional navigation (N about 3.5) with a turn-rate limit, burns out and
   slows, and self-destructs after about 6 s. Proximity fuze about 5 m, lethal inside about 2.5 m, fragment damage on the
   same jet hit points that flak uses (damaged jets smoke and RTB).
4. The target reacts: radar warning ("mud spike"), a beam turn to notch the radar, and chaff. Chaff and notching can break
   the lock, and the missile then flies on unguided. Radio on your own side (SIMULATED) with real brevity words.
5. FX: launch flash and backblast cloud, a thick white booster trail (lighter on LOW and phones), burst flash and puff.
   Missiles in flight capped per tier. API `sam.getState()`. Kill switch `?sam=0`. Theater FX only, labelled SIMULATED;
   never touches market data, army counts, the price-driven front or data-truth labels.

Result (cycle 1): all gates passed; deployed as v12.0.37. Changed in testing: guidance never engaged (fixed the early target-gone check), both battery sites moved to open ground.
