## Cycle 2 → v12.0.23 "Squad firefights: line of sight, terrain and casualties" (v12.1: realistic ballistics and line
## of sight; terrain affects movement and combat)
Today the squads only trade suppression: they fire through hills and walls at anything within 24 m, every shot is the
same, nobody is ever hit, and slopes don't change their pace.
1. **Line of sight** (new `js/theaters/los.js`, `LUNCBattle.los`, `?los=0`): eye-height ray between two points, sampled
   every 1.5 m against the terrain, the Hill 402 ridge, and friendly/enemy walls (1.5 m) and bunkers (1.6 m). Shared so
   other systems can use it later. Cheap: cached per pair for 0.5 s.
2. **Targeting:** squads only engage targets they can see; blocked targets in range show NO LOS in the panel.
3. **Ballistics-lite hit model:** hit chance falls with range (effective 0–12 m, falling to a third at 24 m), +25 %
   firing downhill from 2 m higher, −40 % for a moving shooter, ×0.5 when the target is IN COVER, ×(1 − suppression/2)
   for the shooter, times the weather/night gun modifier from wx-combat.
4. **Casualties:** each man has 2 hp; a hit wounds, two hits down him. Downed men fall and lie for 12 s, then disappear.
   A squad down to 1 man falls back toward its HQ; a squad wiped out sends a radio call and a fresh squad (full
   strength) arrives from its HQ 45 s later. Formation closes up around the survivors.
5. **Terrain on movement:** slope-aware pace (uphill up to −45 %, downhill up to +15 %).
6. UI: men count per squad ("4/5") in the Squads panel and on the big tactical map; kills / losses in the status line.
Game layer only: never removes market units, never moves the front, never touches price or data-truth labels.
`?los=0` restores the v12.0.22 behaviour.
