## Cycle 1 → v12.0.15 "Realism pass, part 3: sea & air motion"
Audit (realism checklist, Motion & physics scored 2/5 for theaters): v12 theater jets yaw like a weather vane — no bank
in turns, the "pitch" term is applied around the world X axis (wrong axis), landings snap the heading 180° in mid-air and
sink 4 m/s onto the runway with no approach, and taxi-in teleports to the parking slot. Ships ride a single sine bob,
never roll or pitch with the ocean shader's waves, flip heading 180° instantly at the end of their patrol lane and
leave no wake.

New module `js/fx/realism-seaair.js` (`LUNCBattle.seaAir`, kill switch `?seaair=0`, also off with `?realism=0`):
1. **Aircraft attitude:** Euler order YZX (yaw → pitch → roll in the plane's own frame). Coordinated-turn bank
   `atan(V·ω/g)` (clamped ±70°, smoothed), pitch from climb rate, slight nose-down on descent. Applies to cruise / fight /
   RTB / takeoff / landing / shoot-down.
2. **Takeoff & landing:** ground roll with rotation at Vr (nose up ~9°) and a climb-out; RTB flies an initial point →
   final-approach fix lined up with the runway, decelerates to approach speed, follows a glide slope with flare, touches
   down, rolls out with braking, then taxis to its parking slot (no teleport).
3. **Ships:** heave / pitch / roll sampled from the same wave function as the ocean shader at bow, stern and beams
   (smaller hulls roll more, response lag/damping); calm-sea swell on LOW where the ocean is flat. U-turns at the end of
   patrol lanes follow a turning circle with heel into the turn instead of a 180° snap. Foam wake (V-shaped, alpha
   texture, fades with speed) and bow spray puffs (MEDIUM+).
4. Tier cost: wakes/spray off on LOW/phone; attitude math is a handful of sin() per ship/jet.
Never touches market data, the front, army counts or labels (theater layer is SIMULATED).
