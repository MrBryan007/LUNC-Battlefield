## Cycle 2 → v12.0.33 "Air ops: gear, afterburners, catapults and carrier traps" (realism checklist, still open after
part 3: "no landing gear or flaps animation, no carrier traps (deck jets RTB to the airbase)")
Theater jets are a fuselage, a delta wing and a fin with nothing underneath: they taxi, land and park on thin air, and
deck jets launch at 14 m/s off a standing start and then fly home to the airbase. New module `js/fx/air-ops.js`:
1. Landing gear on every theater jet: a nose leg and two main legs (struts + wheels) that extend on final approach
   and retract a second after lift-off (animated over ~1.5 s, hidden when stowed), plus a tailhook.
2. Engines: a nozzle glow and an afterburner plume (outer flame + hot core, additive) that follows the throttle: full
   reheat on the takeoff roll, catapult shot and in a dogfight, dim in cruise, off on approach and on the ground. A red
   anti-collision beacon blinks while the engine runs.
3. Catapult launches: a deck jet now sits on the bow catapult and is shot off the deck (about 3 g) with steam venting
   along the track (MEDIUM+), riding the carrier's motion, instead of appearing at 14 m/s.
4. Carrier recoveries: a deck jet goes home to its own carrier while it is still afloat: it joins astern, flies a
   glide slope down the deck centreline (the "meatball"), traps on the wire with a hard arrest (about 2.5 g), taxis
   to a deck spot and rides with the ship, and is struck below after 12–20 s (back into the airbase pool). Too high or
   off the centreline at the ramp → bolter / wave-off and a new pass. A sinking carrier loses the jets on its deck;
   jets still in the pattern divert to the airbase. Radio: "trap, wire N" / "bolter" (SIMULATED, own side).
5. API `airOps.getState()` (gear down/up counts, traps, bolters, cat shots, divert count). Kill switch `?airops=0`
   (also off with `?seaair=0` / `?realism=0`). Theater FX only, SIMULATED, no market/front/data changes.

## Cycle 3 → v12.0.34 "Flak: aimed AA shells with proximity fuzes" (realism checklist, open after part 3: "AA kills are
still a random roll rather than a fuze/proximity model"; combat behavior: realistic ranges and rates of fire)
The airbase AA guns roll a 6 % chance to kill whichever enemy jet is within 60 m and draw a puff at the jet; the HQ
base AA defenses only draw tracers and can never hit anything. New module `js/fx/flak.js`:
1. Guns lead their target: predicted intercept from the jet's heading, speed and climb, the shell's time of flight
   (muzzle velocity ~75 m/s in world units) and gravity drop. Aim dispersion grows with range and in bad weather / at
   night / under cyber (the existing `wxCombat` 'aa' modifier).
2. Real shells: ballistic flight with a faint tracer, a time fuze set to the predicted intercept and a proximity fuze
   (~3 m from an enemy jet). A jinking or turning jet (dogfights) beats the lead more often than a straight flyer.
3. Bursts: a flash and a black flak puff that drifts and lingers; fragment damage falls off with distance (lethal
   inside ~1.5 m, nothing beyond ~5 m). Jets now have hit points: a damaged jet trails smoke and breaks off for home,
   a destroyed one goes down as before. Delayed boom for far bursts via `sfx.explosion`.
4. Both the airbase AA guns and the HQ base AA defenses (base invasions) fire flak, so base AA can now actually shoot
   raiders down. Own-side radio: "hit by flak, RTB" and "splash one" (SIMULATED).
5. Caps per tier (shells in flight: LOW 16, MEDIUM 32, HIGH 48), fewer puffs on LOW / phones. API `flak.getState()`.
   Kill switch `?flak=0` restores the old random roll. Theater FX only, SIMULATED, no market/front/data changes.
