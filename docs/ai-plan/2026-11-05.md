## Cycle 1 → v12.0.57 "Rain and mud on the armies" (realism checklist: Materials — "no wet/muddy variation by weather")
Today rain only darkens the ground and lays wetter tread prints; every tank, gun and soldier stays bone-dry.
1. New module `js/fx/rain-mud.js` owns a slow **wet** (soaks in ~20 s, dries ~60 s) and an even slower **mud** level
   (builds over ~90 s of rain, then cakes and flakes off over ~4 min once it stops).
2. The v12.0.14 camo shader gets two shared uniforms: wet darkens paint and fabric and makes it glossy (lower
   roughness, a touch more env reflection); mud paints a dark-brown band up from the tracks / boots, splashed
   with noise, which stays matte and dries lighter. One shared uniform object → no per-material work, LOW keeps it.
3. MEDIUM+: moving MBTs and SPGs near the camera throw small brown mud spray off their tracks while the ground is wet.
4. Puddles: a few dozen glossy standing-water patches in low ground behind both lines that fill while it rains
   (with ripple rings on HIGH+), and shrink and vanish as it dries. One instanced draw call; none on LOW / phones.
5. Weather panel line shows "mud 40 %". Kill switch `?mud=0` (also off with `?realism=0`). Cosmetic only.

Cycle 1 result: tests passed after one fix. The first shader build declared the mud value inside a block, so the camo
program failed to compile and every camo unit vanished with no page error. The probes now fail on shader compile
errors. Deploying v12.0.57.

## Cycle 2 → v12.0.58 "Flight controls: flaps, control surfaces, speed brake and wingtip vapour"
Roadmap (v12.0.15 sea & air, still open): "flaps/slats animation". Theater jets have gear, hooks and burners, but
the airframe is rigid: nothing moves when a jet banks, pitches, lands or brakes.
1. New module `js/fx/flight-controls.js`, called right after the air-ops pose for every theater jet. It measures the
   delta wing and fin of the airframe and adds hinged parts in the airframe's own paint: trailing-edge elevons,
   leading-edge flaps, a rudder and a dorsal speed brake.
2. Elevons droop as flaps on the takeoff roll, on approach and in the flare, and deflect for pitch (from pitch
   rate) and differentially for roll (from bank rate). Leading-edge flaps droop at low speed and in hard turns. The
   rudder follows the yaw rate in the air and steers on the ground. The speed brake opens on the landing rollout,
   on a fast approach and when a fighter bleeds speed in a dogfight. Hinge rates are limited so nothing snaps.
3. Wingtip vapour trails (MEDIUM+): hard turns in a dogfight pull white vortices off the wingtips, and humid air
   (rain, fog, or still-wet ground from v12.0.57) makes them show at lower g. A budget per tick keeps it cheap.
4. Kill switch `?flightctl=0` (also off with `?seaair=0` / `?realism=0`). Visual only, never touches the sim.

Cycle 2 result: tests passed (the first probe run found the rudder too timid in real turn rates and no leading-edge
droop on final; both tuned). Deploying v12.0.58.
