# Extended session (started 08:44 PT) — back-to-back cycles
Bryan asked for 1–2 h of real work today. Cycles run one after another; each is planned here, built on `ai/2026-10-13-<n>`, gated, then shipped as a patch.

## Cycle 1 → v12.0.2: War economy (v12.1 "resource collection")
- New `js/theaters/economy.js` (game layer). Per-side stockpiles of fuel / ammo / intel.
- Income every 4 s, weighted by market signals and labelled **ESTIMATED · SIMULATED**: fuel ← the side's share of the army (market walls), ammo ← |momentum|, intel ← whoever momentum favours. Captured supply crates add +5 of their kind.
- Spendable by the Commander (Map → WAR SYSTEMS): Recon sweep (intel 6 → reveals 70 u around the front for 20 s), Rearm (ammo 8 → active cooldowns −30 %), Rush drop (fuel 8 → resets the supply-drop call cooldown). The AI side spends too (radio line only).
- Never changes price, armies or the front. `?economy=0` off.

## Cycle 2 → v12.0.3: Tech tree (v12.1)
- New `js/theaters/tech.js`: 5 techs per side, bought with the economy stockpile, 20 s research each, with prerequisites:
  Logistics (fuel 10: income ×1.25) → Field Radar (intel 8: recon sweep lasts 35 s, wider) and Rapid Reload (ammo 12: Commander cooldowns tick 20 % faster) → Air Bridge (fuel 14, needs Field Radar: supply-drop call cooldown halved) and Fortifications (fuel 10 + ammo 10, needs Rapid Reload: unlocks placing defenses, cycle 3).
- Shown as a small tree in Map → WAR SYSTEMS · TECH with research progress. The enemy AI researches too (radio line).
- Game layer only. `?tech=0` off.

## Cycle 3 → v12.0.4: Defensive structures (v12.1)
- New `js/theaters/forts.js`: Bunker (8 fuel + 4 ammo), Turret (6 fuel + 8 ammo), Wall (6 fuel). Needs the Fortifications tech.
- Place: pick a type in Map → WAR SYSTEMS · DEFENSES, then tap the field. Only on your side of the front (snapped back if you tap past it). Max 8 per side (5 on LOW/phone).
- Turrets swivel to the nearest enemy unit within 28 u and fire tracers; bunkers show a muzzle flash. Cosmetic game FX: they never remove units or move the front.
- Enemy AI builds too once it has Fortifications. Structures show on the world-map inset? (stretch). `?forts=0` off.

## Cycle 5 → v12.0.6: Supply lines you can cut + reinforcement waves (v12.1)
- New `js/theaters/lines.js`. Each side's HQ→front supply road (the v12.0 convoy road, z −32) gets two depots (forward + rear) with fuel tanks and a flag.
- Cutting: any strike that lands within 9 u of an enemy depot damages it (Commander strikes route through theaters.onStrike). 3 hits → depot destroyed → that side's line is CUT: its economy income is halved, its convoys stop, its next reinforcement wave is delayed. Repair: 25 s automatically, or 5 fuel to rebuild now (button).
- The enemy AI raids your depots with a bomber flyby now and then.
- Reinforcement waves: every 75 s per side (ammo supply drops still speed things up via the economy), a column of marching troops leaves the HQ along the road to the front and folds into the line. A visual game-layer wave: it never changes the market-driven army count or the front. Wave timers shown in Map → WAR SYSTEMS · LINES.
- `?lines=0` off.

## Cycle 6 → v12.0.7: Sound design pass (v12.1 roadmap "Sound design and music")
- `js/sfx.js`: new looped ambience beds (wind, ocean surf, rain) with smooth gain, a stereo-panned jet pass with a Doppler pitch drop, a radio-blip variant, and `audio()` for modules.

## Cycle 7 → v12.0.8: Fuller fog of war (v12.1 roadmap "Fog of war")
- New `js/theaters/fow.js`: a per-faction visibility grid (8 u cells over the whole world), recomputed twice a second while Spectator full vision is OFF.
- Vision sources: your army (sampled), HQ/airbase radar (90 u; blinded 20 s when the enemy hacks your side), your jets (60), UAV drones (50), defenses (26), supply depots (20), recon-sweep and intel-crate reveals. Your half of the land front stays visible.
- Weather and night shrink vision: fog ×0.6, rain ×0.8, night ×0.7.
- Enemy ships, jets, subs, defenses and reinforcement columns outside vision are hidden; ghost markers show last-known positions on the world-map inset. A soft dark fog layer over unseen ground (one textured plane, cheap on LOW) and a fog overlay on the inset.
- The market-driven armies and the frontline are always shown (data truth). Spectator mode (default ON) = full vision, unchanged.

## Cycle 8 → v12.0.9: Squad commands with cover and flanking (v12.1)
- New `js/theaters/squads.js`: three player squads (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie; 5 riflemen each, 3 on LOW/phone) and three enemy AI squads, as a game layer on top of the market armies.
- Orders (Map → WAR SYSTEMS · SQUADS): Move (pick the squad, then tap the field), Hold, Flank (swings wide along the north or south edge, then hooks into the enemy side of the front), Cover (runs to the nearest friendly wall/bunker/depot; "IN COVER" takes half the incoming fire).
- Squads fire tracers at enemies in range; incoming fire suppresses (slower moves). Morale/suppression bar per squad. Enemy AI squads advance, hold and flank too.
- Cosmetic only: never removes market units or moves the front. `?squads=0` off.

## Cycle 9 → v12.0.10: Spoken radio chatter + HQ announcements
- New `js/theaters/voice.js`. Browser SpeechSynthesis can't be routed into WebAudio, so the radio sound is approximated: lower pitch/faster speech, with WebAudio squelch clicks + band-passed (300–3000 Hz), lightly distorted static before/after and under each line. INTERCEPTED enemy lines get heavier static and a lower voice.
- Events: strikes called, nuke, squad orders, depot cut, air-raid warning, carrier hit, reinforcements (+ETA), tech done, supply drop secured/lost, match start, weather/night changes. HQ market lines use real data only: they say LIVE when the price feed is live, otherwise they're labelled SIMULATED. A few humor lines.
- Priority queue: max one spoken line per ~6 s; announcements beat chatter; stale low-priority lines are dropped.
- Subtitle strip above the dock (callsign + line, INTERCEPTED tag), tap to see the last 8 lines. Mute toggle "Radio voice" in Settings → WORLD; `?voice=0` kill switch; honors the main mute/volume.
