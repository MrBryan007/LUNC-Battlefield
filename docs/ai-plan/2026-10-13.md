## Today's slice: v12.1 part 1 — Supply drops + war resources
One day-sized slice of v12.1, from the roadmap's "Supply drops" bullet:

- New module `js/theaters/supply.js` (game/sim layer, SIMULATED, never touches market data, armies or the frontline).
- A cargo plane flies over a drop zone and releases a parachute crate. The zone gets a colored smoke column, a ground ring and a ping on the world-map inset.
- Contents: fuel, ammo, intel or a power-up.
- A small AI squad from each side races to the crate. A capture ring fills for whichever side is in range; if both are in range it shows CONTESTED and stops filling.
- The winning side banks the resource into a per-side tally (fuel / ammo / intel), shown in a new WAR SYSTEMS block in the Map sheet.
- Effects when the Commander's side wins: an intel drop reveals the area (fog of war is lifted around it for 30 s); an ammo drop knocks 30% off the Commander's active cooldowns (the closest existing equivalent of "next wave", since waves aren't built yet); a power-up gives +25 Moon Shot charge (game-only).
- The Commander can call one in (button in the Map sheet, 60 s cooldown); it drops just behind their side of the front.
- Tier caps: LOW/phone keeps at most 1 active drop and 2 squad runners per side; HIGH up to 2 drops.
- Kill switch `?supply=0`. Also off when `?theaters=0`.
