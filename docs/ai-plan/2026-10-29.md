## Cycle 2 → v12.0.40 "Brevity codes and radio procedure" (realism checklist, audio & radio: "authentic brevity codes")
The radio only reports big events (strikes, cut supply lines, raids). The air war is silent on the channel, and the
Commander's fire missions don't follow call-for-fire procedure. New module `js/theaters/brevity.js`:
1. Air-to-air brevity from real dogfight events in `theaters.js` (hooks only, sim unchanged): BANDIT / TALLY when a fight
   starts, FOX TWO on an IR missile launch, DEFENDING / FLARES when the target decoys, SPLASH ONE on a kill, TRASHED on a
   miss, BINGO / RTB when a sortie heads home, and a "hit, going down" call when an own jet is shot down.
2. Call-for-fire procedure for Commander strikes: SHOT, then SPLASH about five seconds later, then ROUNDS COMPLETE.
3. Plain-English glosses in brackets on the subtitle strip (shown, never spoken), plus a glossary of every brevity word
   heard this session at the bottom of the radio log.
4. Throttle: per-kind cooldowns, own side spoken, enemy side INTERCEPTED at a lower rate, nothing above chatter priority so
   HQ and alerts still win. Labelled SIMULATED. Kill switch `?brevity=0`. Radio/UI only, never touches data.

## Cycle 3 → v12.0.41 "Squad specialists" (v12.3 achievements and unlocks, part 4: "still open: unlocks for squads")
Rank unlocks so far are only cosmetic (fort camo, HQ banners). Squads get rank-unlocked specialist kits, chosen per squad
(Alpha / Bravo / Charlie) in Cmd → service record:
- Rifle team (Recruit): the current squad.
- Marksman (Private): longer reach (28 m instead of 24) and better aim past 12 m.
- Machine gunner (Corporal): heavier suppression on the target, slightly faster fire.
- Medic (Sergeant): gets one downed man back up (1 hp) every 25 s while the squad is still in the fight.
- Engineer (Lieutenant): Cover digs in where it stands (foxhole after 4 s) when no wall or bunker is near, and its cover
  stops more fire (x0.35 instead of x0.5).
- Veteran (Captain): 3 hp a man and shakes off suppression 50% faster.
Each kit also shows on the squad (longer rifle, bigger gun, white medic pack, engineer pack and shovel, veteran beret band)
and in the squad panel. Your squads only; enemy AI squads stay rifle teams. Game layer only: never touches market data,
army counts or the front. ?cosmetics=0 or ?squadkits=0 turns kits off.

Result (cycle 3): all gates passed; deployed as v12.0.41.
