## Cycle 2 → v12.0.26 "Defenses realism pass" (realism checklist: materials, motion, FX, audio for forts)
Cycle 1's screenshot showed the defenses as the least realistic thing on the field: the turret is a mint-green box on
a white drum, walls are flat white blocks (colours are not sRGB-converted, so they render washed out), the turret head
snaps instantly onto a target, there is no muzzle flash or sound, and every shot is a tracer stream.
1. Materials: sRGB-correct colours; a procedural weathered-concrete texture (stains, streaks, chips; generated once,
   none on LOW); faction camo (Bulls olive drab, Bears grey-tan) with a small faction stripe instead of bright
   green/red heads; darker gun metal.
2. Geometry: bunker with two staggered courses of sandbags, sandbags on the roof, a recessed firing slit with a
   concrete lip; turret as a remote weapon station on a concrete pedestal with an armoured shield, a heavy barrel with a
   muzzle brake and an ammo box; walls as Jersey-profile barrier segments (1.5 m, the LOS height) with small gaps.
3. Motion: turret traverse limited to 75°/s and it only fires within 6° of the target; barrel recoil kick.
4. FX: muzzle flash (short additive sprite at the muzzle; one shared flash light on HIGH only), tracers 1-in-5 (a burst
   shows 1 tracer, the rest are only flashes/sound), brass-dust puff on bunkers.
5. Audio: `sfx.gunfire` with distance attenuation from the camera and speed-of-sound delay for far forts (> 120 m).
Kill switch `?fortfx=0` restores the v12.0.25 look and behaviour. Game FX only, no market/front/data changes.

## Cycle 3 → v12.0.27 "Forts under fire: damage, suppression, repair" (v12.1 bunkers/turrets/walls, next depth step)
Forts are invulnerable: a Commander strike, a UAV missile or a torpedo-sized blast next to an enemy bunker does nothing,
squads ignore them, and once built they stand forever. The open item from v12.0.26 is "damage and destruction".
1. Hit points (wall 6, bunker 8, turret 5). Any blast through `theaters.onStrike` (Commander strikes, UAV missiles,
   mortars) within 6 m × √power damages forts, more near the centre. Friendly fire counts, as for the depots.
2. Damage states: below half HP the fort is scorched and smokes; at 0 it's destroyed (fire, smoke, collapse to a dark
   wreck), stops blocking line of sight and frees its slot. The wreck is cleared after 40 s.
3. Suppression: an enemy squad with no squad target but line of sight to a fort in range fires at it. Rifles don't hurt
   concrete, but the crew is suppressed: the fort fires slower and less accurately (up to ×2 cooldown, ×0.5 hit chance),
   decaying over a few seconds.
4. Enemy artillery: the AI side calls a 3-round mortar mission on one of your forts it can see (not under fog of war)
   every 60–90 s, with an "INCOMING" radio warning and visible splashes. Its engineers repair their own forts too.
5. Defenses panel: damaged / destroyed counts and a Repair button (3 fuel, 20 s cooldown) that restores your most
   damaged fort. API: `forts.damageAt(x, z, power)`, `forts.targetsFor(side)`, `forts.suppress(id)`, `forts.repair()`.
Kill switch `?fortdmg=0` restores the v12.0.26 invulnerable forts. Game FX only, no market/front/data changes.
