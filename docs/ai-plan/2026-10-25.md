## Cycle 2 → v12.0.36 "Tread marks: tracks and ruts that persist under vehicles" (realism checklist, terrain:
"height-aware tracks that persist under vehicles", carried over since part 1)
Tanks and self-propelled guns roll across the field and leave nothing behind. New module `js/fx/tracks.js`:
1. Every MBT lays a pair of track prints (about 0.35 m wide, 2.1 m apart) and every SPG a pair of narrower wheel ruts
   as it moves, a new print roughly every metre of travel. Prints follow the terrain: height from `terrainHeight` and
   tilted to the local slope, so they hug berms and craters instead of floating or cutting in.
2. One InstancedMesh ring buffer, one draw call. Caps per tier (LOW / phones 500, MEDIUM 1400, HIGH 2800, ULTRA
   4000 prints). Prints age over minutes (LOW 2.5 min, others 7 min): they lighten toward the soil colour and thin
   out at the end of their life, then the slot is reused.
3. Weather: in rain the prints are darker, wetter and slightly wider (mud). Nothing in the ocean or on the beach.
4. Wrecks and hidden units lay nothing, and a jump of more than 6 m (respawn) starts a fresh line.
5. API `tracks.getState()`. Kill switch `?tracks=0` (also off with `?realism=0`). Cosmetic only, never touches
   market data, army counts, the price-driven front or data-truth labels.
