## Cycle 1 → v12.0.29 "Fort crews and combat engineers" (realism checklist: the open fort item "crew figures,
engineers walking out to repair")
Forts are empty concrete: nobody mans the turret, and Repair instantly heals a fort from across the map.
1. Crew figures (procedural, shared geometry, faction camo + helmet): a gunner behind each turret's shield that turns
   with the head, and a sentry in the trench behind each bunker. A suppressed crew ducks (crouch scale); a wrecked
   fort's crew is gone ("crew pulling back").
2. Combat engineers: Repair (yours and the AI's) now sends a two-man engineer team on foot from 35 m behind the fort.
   They walk over the terrain at about 3.5 m/s, work for 6 s (dust and sparks), and only then is the fort restored.
   Mortars / strikes within 4 m of an engineer kill him; if both are killed the repair fails (fuel is spent). If the
   fort is destroyed before they arrive, they turn back. One team per fort.
3. Defenses panel: Repair shows "engineers en route / repairing", and the status shows engineers lost.
   API: `forts.engineers()`, `forts.crewEnabled()`. "Hold the Line" counts completed repairs.
Kill switch `?fortcrew=0` restores v12.0.28 (no crew, instant repair). Game FX only, no market/front/data changes.

Result (cycle 1): all gates passed (83 JS files, asset-lifecycle 33/33, smoke 26/26, HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / iPhone 0 page
errors, the new crew probe on HIGH, iPhone and `?fortcrew=0`, the v12.0.27 fort-damage probe on MEDIUM). Changed while
building: the first crew colours rendered bright lime against the armies' camo, so crews now share the armies' camo

## Cycle 2 → v12.0.30 "Navy detail: funnels, exhaust, radar, deck markings" (realism checklist: "no ship smoke stacks
or exhaust", toy-box ships)
The warships are plain boxes: no funnels, no masts, no waterline paint, and the carrier deck is a blank grey slab.
New module `js/fx/naval-detail.js` decorates every ship the theaters spawn (shared geometry/materials, no assets):
1. Destroyers / battleships: raked funnels with black caps (one / two), a lattice-style mast with a yardarm and a
   rotating air-search radar, a dark boot-top waterline band, secondary AA mounts and lifeboats.
2. Carrier: funnel on the island, island mast + rotating radar, a dark boot-top, and painted deck markings (dashed
   centre line, the angled landing-area lines, a foul line and an elevator outline), all sRGB-correct.
3. Exhaust: funnels trail smoke that thickens with speed and turning (MEDIUM+ and not on phones); a burning or sinking
   ship stops making exhaust and its radar stops turning.
4. API `navalDetail.getState()` (ships decorated, exhaust puffs, radars). Kill switch `?navdetail=0` (also off with
   `?realism=0` / `?seaair=0`). Theater FX only, SIMULATED, no market/front/data changes.

Result (cycle 2): all gates passed (84 JS files, asset-lifecycle 33/33, smoke 27/27, HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / iPhone 0 page

## Cycle 3 → v12.0.31 "Hot-seat: two commanders, one device" (v12.2 Multiplayer, part 1: hot-seat first)
The roadmap's v12.2 starts with hot-seat: turn handoff, a camera and fog per player, and a pass-the-device screen.
Nothing of v12.2 exists yet. New module `js/ui/hotseat.js`:
1. Setup card in the Cmd sheet (and `?hotseat=1` opens it): Player 1 commands the Bulls, Player 2 the Bears; turn
   length 60 / 90 / 120 s; 2 / 3 / 5 rounds.
2. Turns: during a turn the game runs with that player's side as the Commander (`commander.setSide`). Each player
   has their own Commander session (score, combo, Moon Shot / nuke charge) and their own strike cooldowns
   (`commander.useSession`), their own camera position, and the fog of war follows the active side.
3. Pass-the-device screen: at the end of a turn the battle pauses and an opaque cover hides the field (so the next
   player doesn't see the other side's fog view), with the scoreboard and "Bears commander, tap when ready".
4. A turn chip in the top bar (player, side, time left, End turn). After the last round: a results screen with each
   player's score, hits and strikes and the winner, then Play again / Exit. Exit restores your own side and session.
5. Game layer only: the market-driven front, armies and data labels are never touched; scores are game points.
Kill switch `?hotseat=0`. API `hotseat.start(opts)`, `endTurn()`, `stop()`, `getState()`.
